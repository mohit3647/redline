# 02 — Collect first-person freelancer accounts

Status: ready-for-human

PRD §8 gap 2. The segment the product is built for has survey statistics and no
voices: the research produced zero verbatim quotes from a freelancer about a
contract clause.

Cause is known and is a hard constraint, not a research failure: Reddit is
unreachable from this environment — WebSearch rejects the domain and WebFetch
cannot load it — so r/freelance, r/smallbusiness and r/legaladvice were never
searched. Any route that goes through Reddit tooling will fail the same way.

Marked ready-for-human because the useful version is twenty freelancers
describing a contract that hurt them, which is outreach rather than search. It
is also the cheapest remaining research: it would either confirm the three
anchor clauses in PRD §5 or overturn them, and right now they rest on judgement
alone (see issue 03 and PRD §8 gap 1).
